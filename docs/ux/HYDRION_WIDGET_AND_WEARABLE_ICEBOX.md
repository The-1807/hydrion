# Widget And Wearable Icebox

The following are intentionally deferred:

- iOS WidgetKit and iOS home-screen widgets pending real Apple-device validation
- iOS Live Activities
- Apple Watch and other wearable integrations
- Android lock-screen widget expansion
- large or complex widget families
- active-challenge and challenge-specific widget families
- advanced widget configuration, themes, and per-widget amounts
- version 2 wearable concepts

These items require separate product scope, platform validation, privacy review, and maintenance budgets. They are not part of the Android Daily Progress and Quick Log MVP.
