# Widget And Wearable Icebox

The following are intentionally deferred:

- general Daily Progress widget
- general Quick Log widget
- iOS WidgetKit and iOS home-screen widgets
- iOS Live Activities
- Apple Watch
- Wear OS
- Android lock-screen widget expansion
- large or complex widget families
- advanced widget configuration and themes
- social competition and public challenge leaderboards
- new challenge families
- extensive challenge-image redesign

This sprint includes only the Android Active Challenge widget. Deferred items
require separate product scope, privacy review, platform validation, and
maintenance budgets.
