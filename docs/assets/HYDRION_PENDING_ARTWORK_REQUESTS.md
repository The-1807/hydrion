- Keep essential subjects inside the central 80 percent for card and hero crops.
- Use clean editorial illustration, soft natural lighting, defined edges, and

- Ensure subjects remain legible on light and dark surfaces.
- Do not include words, logos, brands, school or employer identifiers, uniforms,

  measurement imagery, or personal data.
